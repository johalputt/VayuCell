# VayuCell trademark policy

**This policy is separate from the code licence and does not restrict forking.**

The Apache-2.0 licence grants you broad rights to the *code*. It does not grant
rights to the *name*. That separation is deliberate and exists for exactly one
reason: so a user can tell whether the thing they are about to leave plugged in,
unattended, in their home, is the project that made the safety commitments in
`CHARTER.md` Article III.

## You may, without asking

- Fork the project, modify it, and distribute your fork under a different name.
- State truthfully that your work is "based on VayuCell" or "a fork of VayuCell".
- Use the name to refer to this project — in articles, talks, comparisons,
  packaging metadata and academic work.
- Redistribute unmodified releases under the name.

## You may not, without permission

- Name a modified distribution "VayuCell", or a name likely to be confused with
  it, in a way that implies it is the official project.
- Use the logo or visual identity to endorse an unrelated product.
- Imply that a fork's safety behaviour is this project's safety behaviour.

## The one that actually matters

A fork that **weakens or removes the Battery Safety Governor**, or that reports a
safety property it does not verify, **may not use this name.** Charter Article IX
already states that such an implementation is not VayuCell whatever it is called;
this policy is the mechanism that makes it enforceable.

That is the whole purpose of holding a mark on a project that is otherwise
maximally permissive. Everything else here is negotiable.
